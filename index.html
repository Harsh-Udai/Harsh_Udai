Research

My work focuses on compositional generalization in machine learning. I treat vision–language models not only as capable systems but as imperfect approximations of multimodal cognition, and I investigate their internal representations, shortcuts and failure modes. Using mechanistic interpretability, I aim to reverse-engineer these models and turn the insights into training objectives and architectures that are more modular, transparent and reliable.
